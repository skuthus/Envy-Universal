The Index

            

            

              
              
            

            
            

              Every folder has a color from the moment it exists. Hover a
              note's folder dot to see the folder's name; right-click the dot
              (or name chip) to pick the color you want.
            

          


          

            
Templates

            
            

            
          


          

            
Trash

            
            

              The whole Trash folder is swept into the system Trash this often,
              counting from the last time it was emptied — checked when Envy
              starts and hourly while it runs.
            

            

              
              
            

          


          

            
Note list

            
            
            
            
            
            
            

              For a note in a subfolder: a dot in the folder's color, the
              folder's name as a chip, or nothing. Needs “Show items in
              subfolders”.
            

            
            

              A note's folder dot or name chip sits just after the title by
              default. Turn this off to move it before the title. The Inbox
              mark always stays on the left.
            

            
            
            

              Parks your pinned notes (up to three) just below the search bar
              so they stay reachable no matter how far you scroll. Off, they
              sort to the top of the list as before and scroll away with it.
            

            
            

              The Inbox is a holding pen for captures you haven't filed. With it
              on, notes in an Inbox/ folder are fleeting: a count sits beside
              the search box, an amber mark sets them apart in the list, and
              inbox: finds them. Turn it off and there's no inbox
              at all: no count, no marker, no inbox: operator, and
              any notes in an Inbox/ folder are treated as ordinary notes.
            

            
            

              Everything you write begins as a fleeting note, and filing it
              into The Index becomes a deliberate act — including notes created
              by following a [[link]] that doesn't exist yet. Notes
              made from a template are unaffected, since those arrive with
              structure you chose deliberately.
            

            
          


          

            
Editor

            
            
            
            
            

              A bare URL renders as a compact pill of just its domain. Purely
              visual and entirely offline — the note still holds the full URL,
              and it shows in full the moment your cursor enters it.
            

            
            
            
            
          


          

            
Footer

            
            
            
            
            

              Word and character counts are the open note's; note and folder
              counts are the whole vault's. The folder count appears only when
              subfolders are shown.
            

            
            
            
            
          


          

            
Shortcuts

            

            

            

              Wayland lets no app register a hotkey that works from other apps,
              so the from-anywhere chords are not listed here. Show/Hide Envy is
              Ctrl+Alt+Return through the Hyprland bind under
              System; a bind of your own that runs
              envynote --pinned does the same for the pinned
              note. Keep on Top is in the bar icon's menu.

System

            
            
            
            
            
            

              Off, the window floats. Both apply straight away to the windows
              that are open.
            

            
            

              Adds one line to ~/.config/hypr/bindings.lua so
              Ctrl+Alt+Return shows or hides Envy from any app,
              Ctrl+Alt+C centers it, and the window opens
              floating. Off removes that line again. A line you added yourself
              is left alone. The bar icon does the same on click either way.

Import

            
            

              Plug in your Kindle and pull its highlights and typed notes into
              the Inbox as fleeting notes, one per highlight, titled by the
              quote's first words, with the book as a [[link]]. Envy remembers
              what it has already imported, so re-importing only ever adds
              what's new.
            

            

              A Kindle is detected whether it mounts as a USB drive or, like
              every recent Paperwhite, connects over MTP: Omarchy's desktop
              reads MTP devices directly. If yours still isn't seen, copy its
              My Clippings.txt to this machine and use “Choose Clippings
              File…”.
            

            
            

              A book without page numbers falls back to its location, so a
              title's never left bare unless you choose None.
